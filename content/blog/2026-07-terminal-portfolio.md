I decided to rebuild my portfolio as a terminal-inspired website. Here's what I learned along the way.

## The Concept

Instead of a typical "developer dashboard" layout, I wanted something that feels like a workspace — a place where commands become navigation and every page tells a story.

## The Approach

- **Asymmetric layouts** — breaking away from centered grids
- **CSS art** — geometric shapes instead of emoji icons
- **Selective animation** — only key moments animate, not everything at once
- **Connecting thread** — a visual thread running through all sections
- **Human-crafted feel** — avoiding the "AI-generated" pattern

## What's Next

There's still more to explore — a blog section, interactive command playground, and a guestbook for visitors to leave messages. The portfolio is a living project.
